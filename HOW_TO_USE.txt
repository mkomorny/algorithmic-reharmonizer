HOW TO USE: reharmonization-engine
==================================================

PURPOSE
  Upgrades simple, standard, or repetitive chord progressions into 3 distinct
  sophisticated variations: Neo-Soul & R&B, Modern Jazz (Tritone Sub), and
  Cinematic Modal Interchange with step-by-step harmonic explanations.

CAPABILITIES
  - Flavor 1: Neo-Soul & R&B (lush 9ths/11ths/13ths + chromatic half-step passing chords)
  - Flavor 2: Modern Jazz (tritone substitutions, altered dominants, and chromatic basslines)
  - Flavor 3: Cinematic Modal Interchange (borrowed minor iv, bVI major 7ths, and backdoor bVII)
  - Side-by-side original vs. variation progression comparisons
  - Explains the exact music theory "secret sauce" behind every substitution
  - Provides note-by-note chord voicings and spellings for keys and guitar
  - Seamlessly bridges to ableton-midi-chord-builder for 1-click MIDI clip generation

USE CASES
  - A 4-chord loop feels too predictable and needs sophisticated passing chords
  - You want to transform a simple pop chord sequence into a soulful D'Angelo or Robert Glasper groove
  - You need dramatic, emotional tension and bittersweet release in a bridge or chorus
  - You want to replace a harsh V7 dominant cadence with a silky tritone step-down
  - You need modal borrowing to evoke nostalgia or cinematic wonder

IDEAS
  - Chain with chord-progression-recommender: find statistically popular chord loops, then reharmonize them
  - Bridge to ableton-midi-chord-builder to export Drop-2 or Jazz Open voicings into Ableton Live
  - Mix and match bars between Neo-Soul and Modal Interchange variations for a hybrid progression
  - Use the minor iv chord substitution to write poignant intros or outro fades
  - Turn basic trap and lo-fi beats into sophisticated R&B arrangements

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  Take this chord progression (C - Am - F - G) and give me 3 spicy reharmonizations.

  Make this chord loop sound like Neo-Soul.

  How can I add smooth jazz tritone substitutions to this chorus?

  Add emotional borrowed chords from the parallel minor to this progression: F - Dm - Bb - C.

  Reharmonize Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7 using tritone substitutions and passing chords.

  Model: [Runs reharmonization engine]
         Original:  C -> Am -> F -> G

         Voicing:   D7alt = D - F# - C - Eb - Bb
         [Offers 1-click Ableton MIDI clip generation via ableton-midi-chord-builder]

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Parse the input chords, tonic center, and key signature.
  2. Compute 3 distinct variations: Neo-Soul, Modern Jazz Tritone Sub, and Cinematic Modal Interchange.
  3. Format a clear comparison table contrasting original chords with each variation.
  4. Detail the voice-leading rationale and music theory principles behind the substitutions.
  5. Provide note-by-note chord spellings and offer to export MIDI clips directly into Ableton Live.
